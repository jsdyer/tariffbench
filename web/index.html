Which rates are in this estimate?

Standard residential service only. BC minimum daily charges are included. Nova Scotia's published combined energy price already includes its listed fuel, operational and efficiency charges; they are not added a second time. NS equipment-based time-of-day service and enrollment-limited pilots are not modelled.

Electricity costs here exclude taxes unless you enter an adjustment, rebates, special service charges and future rate changes. Your bill and the utility's applicable tariff take precedence. For billing periods crossing a rate change, split your estimate at the effective date.

Rate maintenance and privacy

Your device names, wattages, bill inputs and scenario files are processed in this browser. They are not uploaded. Opening the page loads public application files and the site's aggregate visit counter; calculator inputs are not included. Saved scenarios stay in this browser until you delete them.
